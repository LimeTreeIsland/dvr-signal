# Legal baseline and evidence register

Status: initial research map, not a complete or approved legal ruleset.
Prepared 2026-09-26. Rule seeds remain disabled pending review.

## Authority and evidence are different dimensions

Authority types: federal statute; federal regulation; Washington statute;
Washington regulation; DVR policy; official guidance; operational status; project
methodology. A manual or state plan does not automatically establish a statutory
deadline. Current operational status needs its own dated official source.

Evidence types: participant report; document-verified fact; agency response;
adjudicated finding. Verifying a document verifies what it establishes, not every
allegation within it. Preserve disputes and unknowns. Do not present allegations
as adjudicated facts or color them as automatic findings of illegality.

## Healthy process map

| Stage | Intended function | Research anchor | Timing treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Application | Recognize an application and identify information needed | 34 CFR 361.41(b) | Verify application trigger; referral is not automatically application |
| Eligibility | Individual eligibility and priority assessment | 34 CFR 361.41(b), 361.42 | Federal 60-day framework includes exceptions; seed needs full review |
| Assessment | Use existing information; obtain additional assessment when needed | 34 CFR 361.45(b), (f) | No invented universal standalone assessment deadline |
| IPE | Individualized written plan, informed choice, signatures and copy | 34 CFR 361.45–361.46 | 90-day framework and agreed specific-date extension; applicability review needed |
| Services | Deliver agreed services and evaluate progress | 34 CFR 361.45(a), 361.46, 361.48 | Use individualized plan timelines; no universal time-to-employment promise |
| Employment | Evaluate chosen outcome and need for ongoing support | 34 CFR 361.56 | Employment is not by itself successful closure |
| Closure | Apply the relevant closure conditions and notices | 34 CFR 361.43–361.44, 361.56 | Distinguish closure types and applicable conditions |

The linear sequence is an explanatory spine, not a universal legal sequence.
Assessment can occur before eligibility and during plan development. Show order
of selection/waitlist conditions, annual IPE review, amendments, appeals,
ineligibility, withdrawal and other closure routes. Check current Washington
operations before applying a waitlist-related timing interpretation.

## Verified source-text correction

[WAC 388-891A-0211](https://app.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=388-891A-0211)
was checked against the official live text on 2026-09-26. For covered denials,
the ten-working-day response period starts with receipt of the request. The
rule addresses oral and written responses and permits a written explanation
of needed supplemental information and additional time. It is not a clock
starting on the denial date. Working-day calculation and case applicability
still require review before software can produce a deadline.

## Source registry and review gate

See `data/washington/authorities.yaml` for official source entry points and source
status. Public source URLs belong in the repo; private evidence and supplied
case documents do not. Do not infer the manual's edition from its filename.

To approve a rule: read current official pinpoint text and relevant definitions;
compare applicable state/federal provisions; document exceptions, event anchor,
date precision and counting rules; distinguish source verification from legal
interpretation; record reviewer, reviewed date and approval reference; test
boundary/unknown cases; obtain maintainer approval. Re-review after authority
changes and before launch. Conflicts block activation rather than being silently
resolved in favor of a more dramatic dashboard result.

Records research must distinguish personal record access under 34 CFR 361.38(c)
and Washington DVR rules from public-records requests under chapter 42.56 RCW.
The supplied chapter 34.05 RCW file is not a substitute for the PRA. Add and
verify the missing PRA authorities before enabling that route's timing output.
