# Participant tool safety

Status: implementation contract. Tool definitions are disabled scaffolds.

## Shared result requirements

Every result must show what the participant entered, what the rule says, why it
may apply, what remains unknown, the official source and last-reviewed date,
and a practical next step. Use text/icons alongside color. Never invent facts,
make a diagnosis, guarantee a benefit, assert a violation, or impersonate counsel.

Generate editable requests, not automatic submissions. Copy, print and save are
explicit user actions. Names/contact placeholders may be completed locally and
must never be copied into the research survey. Explain the site's independence
and limits without burying the useful result under boilerplate.

## Five tools

| Tool | Intended output | Critical guard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Records Router | Own case record, public-records route, both, or clarification | Separate legal routes, exemptions and timelines; verify destinations |
| Accommodation Builder | Barrier → requested change → communication preferences → editable request | Diagnosis detail optional; no eligibility guarantee or automatic legal conclusion |
| Written Decision + Deadline Checker | Request for written reasons/rights; verified calculation when possible | Correct receipt/service trigger and missing-fact handling |
| Challenge a DVR Decision | Options and organized questions/draft | No assumed tolling; independent routes and deadlines; no merits prediction |
| Pathway Navigator | Current stage, expected steps, source-based questions | Assessment/order-of-selection branches; incomplete facts remain unknown |

For own records, research 34 CFR 361.38(c) including release conditions and
correction procedure. For policies/correspondence, research the applicable
Washington PRA route; documents can overlap and third-party information may be
withheld. Do not promise that every internal email is in the case-service file.

## Go-live gates

1. Maintainer approves source-based logic and templates; no pending rule drives
   a deadline or legal classification.
2. Test routing, unknowns, exception paths, precision and date boundaries.
3. Verify current official contact methods; show checked date and official link.
4. Test keyboard/screen-reader/mobile/zoom; provide accessible generated text.
5. Inspect network activity and storage to confirm help inputs stay local.
6. Inspect public bundles and logs for PII, credentials and raw survey data.
7. Publish correction/reporting process and version identifiers.

An expired or unverified source must yield a clear unavailable/review-needed
state. Other verified informational functions can remain usable independently.
